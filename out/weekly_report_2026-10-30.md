- **AI-infra linkage:** Its MOCVD tools make the InP/GaAs laser epi wafers behind EML/VCSEL transceivers in AI data-center optical interconnects. Q3 order intake of about EUR275m (vs EUR124m a year earlier, consensus EUR214.6m) was attributed by management to laser equipment for AI data-center communications.
- **Net thesis:** AIXTRON is a real but second-order AI play. Its MOCVD tools make the EML/VCSEL laser epi behind AI optical transceivers. The 15 Oct Q3 prelim (orders ~EUR 275m vs ~EUR 214.6m consensus median, ~48% gross margin, ~31% EBIT margin) shows a genuine cycle turn off a trough. But FY26 guidance is only flat on FY25, the AI share (35-50%) is an analyst estimate, and the stock at ~67x 2026 and ~30x 2027 EPS already prices a good part of the recovery. The setup is positive on estimate revisions, but upside depends on 2027 earnings, and that depends on order conversion and on whether AI optics capex holds.
- **Catalyst:** Full Q3 2026 statement confirmed the preliminary figures: orders about EUR275m, revenue about EUR184m, EBIT about EUR57m (31% margin), FY26 guidance unchanged (revenue about EUR560m, 17-20% EBIT margin). Preliminary numbers were released Oct 15, before the window. I could not retrieve the Oct 29 statement itself; this rests on a secondary report that the stock reacted calmly. (2026-10-29) — [source](https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/aixtron-aktie-hoffnungsschimmer-nach-zahlen-837140)
- **AI-infra rev share (first read):** Not disclosed. First read: roughly 40-60% of orders now tied to AI optical lasers (estimate, low confidence). The rest is power electronics and LEDs.
- **Key risk:** The Q3 order spike proves to be lumpy and cyclical. Orders push out or are cancelled, or AI optics and hyperscaler capex wobbles. A high-beta stock on a ~30x 2027 multiple would then de-rate. The Q3 margin is mix-driven and guidance implies a Q4 fall-back. Customer concentration, competition from Veeco and Chinese MOCVD makers, and the repeated SiC and Micro-LED disappointments add to this.
